### 🧩 **Research Questions**

1. **Market Overview & Demand**

   * What is the **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**, **Serviceable Available Market (SAM)**, and **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)** for \[product/category]? *(Define acronyms clearly)*
   * What public data sources (e.g., \[example: Google Trends, Similarweb, Crunchbase, Twitter mentions]) support **quantifiable demand**?
   * Is this a **red ocean** (crowded, saturated, mature market) or a **blue ocean** (emerging, underserved niche)?

2. **Competitive Analysis**

   * Identify top competitors such as \[Competitor 1], \[Competitor 2], \[Competitor 3], and \[Competitor 4].
   * Create a **feature matrix** comparing:

     * \[Key feature 1]
     * \[Key feature 2]
     * \[Monetization method]
     * \[User experience or audience type]
   * Include a **SWOT analysis** for each and one for the overall market segment.

3. **\[Technology or Trend]-Driven Differentiation**

   * Where can \[technology, e.g. “AI Agents” or “autonomous tools”] provide **disruption or advantage**? Examples might include:

     * \[Use Case 1]
     * \[Use Case 2]
     * \[Use Case 3]

4. **Customer Profile**

   * Define the **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** for \[product/niche]. Include:

     * **Psychographics** (e.g., motivations, frustrations)
     * **Behavior** (e.g., how they evaluate tools, content they consume)
     * **Buying triggers** (e.g., \[pain points, time-based urgency, budget cycles])
   * Recommend marketing strategies across:

     * **Paid Ads**: \[Platforms, angles]
     * **Organic Content**: \[Channels, keywords]
     * **Referral/Affiliate**: \[Tactics or incentive structures]

5. **Business Model Design**

   * What would be a **differentiated business model** for \[this solution]? Examples might include:

     * \[SaaS, Marketplace, Freemium, API-based, Credit System]
   * Describe:

     * **Revenue Streams**: \[e.g., subscriptions, ads, affiliate, consulting]
     * **User Flow**: \[Onboarding, usage pattern, upsell opportunities]

6. **Traffic & Profitability Projections**

   * Estimate **traffic needed to break even** using industry-standard benchmarks:

     * \[Conversion rate %, CPM, CAC, LTV, etc.]
   * Outline basic **cost structure** assumptions:

     * \[Hosting, AI infra, support, content creation]
